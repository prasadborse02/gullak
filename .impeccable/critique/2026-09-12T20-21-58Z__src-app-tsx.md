## Priority Issues

- [P0] Import JSON silently replaces all data (src/store.ts importJSON, src/screens/Dashboard.tsx). Fix: confirm with counts, auto-export current state first, inline error instead of alert(). Command: harden.
- [P1] On the phone the primary action is below the fold (~1000px at 390px) and the dashboard card clips the next action. Fix: Next deposit under the title on mobile, pot 120px, drop % and bar; dashboard card as two full-width lines. Command: adapt.
- [P1] Irreversible taps with no confirm/undo: Spend, mini-goal ✕ delete beside reorder arrows, reward delete. Fix: inline confirm on Spend with balance after; 5s Undo in toast; move mini-goal delete behind edit. Command: harden.
- [P1] Text contrast below AA across the palette (ink-3 2.8:1, clay button 3.9:1, coin-2 3.2:1). Fix: darken --color-ink-3 to ~#7d6f64, adjust clay/coin-2 tokens in src/index.css. Command: audit.
- [P2] Completion moment is the least designed state: after the burst the page still shows quick-add, ↑↓✕, "Remaining 0 coins"; addMini on a completed goal keeps completedAt. Fix: completed receipt with one button to the wallet; hide quick-add/reorder; clear completedAt on add. Command: delight.
